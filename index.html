THE CAOS PRINCIPLE

Not an encyclopedia. A portal.

CAOS does not claim to own, reproduce or certify all human knowledge. It connects people to original sources: research collections, public datasets, scientific institutions, libraries and open tools. Knowledge remains distributed; discovery becomes easier.
