# Initial source selection

This first directory is a seed collection, not a complete or ranked representation of global knowledge. It prioritizes public research infrastructure, institutional portals, scholarly discovery tools, datasets, repositories and technical standards.

Useful reference guides consulted while assembling the initial seed list:

- U.S. National Library of Medicine, Electronic Databases & Directories: https://www.nlm.nih.gov/services/databases_subject.html
- University of Florida Libraries, Open Access Research and Resources: https://guides.uflib.ufl.edu/oar/repositories
- World Bank Group Library, Free and Open Access resources: https://libguides.worldbank.org/open_access/databases
- UNESCO, Open Science Knowledge Sharing Index: https://www.unesco.org/en/open-science/knowledge-sharing
- European Commission, Open Science and Open Access repositories: https://ec-library-europa-eu.libguides.com/open-science/finding-open-access-research/repositories
- University of California, Berkeley Library, Freely Available Science Resources: https://guides.lib.berkeley.edu/FreeResources/sciences

## Curation notes

- A free-to-search database does not necessarily provide free full text for every record.
- Preprint repositories may contain research that has not undergone peer review.
- Data and software reuse is governed by each item's license and terms.
- Institutional resources can change URLs, coverage and access requirements.
- Links are external; CAOS does not host or control their content.

The initial records are stored in `data/sources.json`. Contributors should add sources with accurate descriptions and access notes.
